# Shared gauge rules

Volt and Tahoe use the same dial when the screen has one.

The filled part of the 300° arc is the color of the current reading. A reading in the red part of the scale paints the whole filled arc in that red. A reading in the green part paints the whole filled arc green. The track does not keep a green start when the needle is in the red.

Each scale ends on that vehicle's own limit. On the Volt, output ends at 111 kW and 111 kW is the reddest. Do not copy 111 kW, 94 MPGe, or 40 mpg onto the Tahoe. Use the Tahoe's limits. Red is the bad end: high for power and engine speed, low for voltage and economy.

The number sits on the vertical center of the circle. The unit sits just below that center and does not push the number up.

The header mark is the ink of the logo at about two and a half times the old word height, still inside the one header row.

A trip-reset control uses a dark metal face and a lamp ring. The face stays metal. Only the ring lights. Do not copy the Volt engine-off command onto the Tahoe.
